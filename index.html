Technical Capabilities

Our Services

Detailed engineering, installation, testing, and maintenance services for commercial and industrial facilities.

Fire Hydrant System

High-pressure water supply networks engineered for rapid firefighting access across multi-storey buildings, warehouses, and industrial plants.

Key Components: Main & jockey fire pumps, MS/GI hydrant riser piping, single/double landing valves, hose reels, courtyard hydrants, and pressure gauges.

Fire Alarm System

Early-warning detection and occupant notification systems designed for rapid zone identification and safe evacuation.

Key Components: Addressable & conventional FACP panels, optical smoke detectors, heat detectors, manual call points, hooters, and FRLS loop cabling.

Fire Sprinkler System

Automated thermal-activated water distribution networks providing 24/7 localized fire control across commercial floors and storage bays.

Key Components: Pendent & upright sprinkler heads, wet riser branch piping, installation control valves, flow switches, and zone isolation valves.

Fire Suppression System

Engineered suppression solutions for server rooms, electrical switchgear enclosures, and sensitive industrial equipment.

Key Components: Cylinder banks, discharge manifolds, release control panels, cross-zone detection, and room integrity verification.
