How We Work

Our Process

A clear six-step workflow from first site visit to long-term maintenance.

Site Evaluation

Assess the building, hazards and available water supply to determine system requirements.

Planning & System Design

Layout of pipes, hydrants, detectors, sprinklers and valves — checked against applicable codes.

Equipment Selection

Detectors, sprinklers, panels and notification devices chosen to suit the hazard and design.

Installation

Piping, wiring and equipment installed per design and manufacturer instructions.

Testing & Commissioning

Hydrostatic, flow and functional testing before final connections and handover.

Maintenance & Inspection

Regular maintenance tasks and annual inspection to keep the system reliable.
