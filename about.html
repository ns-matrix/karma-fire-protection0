Company Profile

A Fire Protection Consultant Built Around Building Safety

Karma Fire Protection LLP, established in 2024, offers solutions for safeguarding assets and ensuring building safety. Our comprehensive services include the installation and maintenance of state-of-the-art fire hydrant systems, advanced fire alarm systems, efficient fire sprinkler systems and innovative fire suppression systems.

  • System planning, design and equipment selection
  • Installation of piping, hydrants, detectors and sprinklers
  • Testing, commissioning and final system verification
  • Maintenance, inspection and annual checks

Our Expertise

Four Systems, One Responsibility

We work across the four core fire protection systems found in commercial and industrial buildings.

Fire Hydrant Systems

Water source, pumping station, distribution pipes, hydrants, valves and fittings — planned, installed and tested.

Fire Alarm Systems

Smoke, heat and flame detectors, manual pull stations, control panels, horns and strobes.

Fire Sprinkler Systems

Water supply, pumping, distribution pipes, sprinklers and control valves with correct spacing and coverage.

Fire Suppression Systems

Suppression solutions for specialised hazards. Detailed system specifications provided on request.

Maintenance & Inspection

Installed Systems Are Only Safe When Maintained

Regular maintenance keeps firefighting systems reliable and helps prevent accidental discharge, system failure and compliance issues.

  • System reliability — regular inspection and lubrication of valves
  • Issue detection — find potential problems before they cause failure
  • Annual inspection — verify systems meet applicable codes
  • Extended lifespan — reduce the need for major repairs
