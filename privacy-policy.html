Last Updated: November 25, 2026

At Karma Fire Protection LLP, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how we keep it secure.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or contact us for fire protection solutions, we may collect:

  • Enquiry Information: Name, phone number, email address, company name, building type, project location, requirements, and preferred contact method submitted through our contact form.
  • Anonymous Analytics Data: Anonymous visitor identifier (UUID), session identifier, visited pages, landing page, referring URL, marketing attribution (UTM parameters), device category (desktop/mobile/tablet), and timestamps.

We do not collect sensitive financial information, government identity numbers, or invasive browser fingerprints. Full IP addresses are hashed using one-way cryptographic SHA-256 for abuse prevention and are never stored in raw form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for genuine business purposes:

  • To prepare and provide quotations, proposals, and consultation on fire protection systems.
  • To contact you regarding your specific enquiry via phone, WhatsApp, or email.
  • To improve website performance, user experience, and service clarity.
  • To protect our website infrastructure against automated spam, denial-of-service, and malicious attacks.

3. First-Party Analytics & Cookies

With optional analytics consent, we record page visits, approximate active time, clicks on links and buttons, scroll-depth thresholds, form-start and submission events (without form field values), and coarse device category. Analytics use random pseudonymous visitor/session identifiers and are not used to follow you across third-party websites. Common email and phone patterns in event labels are masked. You can change your choice using the Cookie settings control shown on the website.

4. Data Retention & Security

Website analytics logs are automatically retained for 90 days, after which they are permanently deleted through automated retention sweeps. Enquiry records are maintained securely in an encrypted environment to service existing customer contracts and warranty records.

5. Sharing of Information

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is only accessible to authorized engineers and staff at Karma Fire Protection LLP.

6. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal enquiry data at any time. To make a request, please contact us at:
