Service 01

Fire Hydrant System

A fire hydrant system is a network of pipes and hydrants that provides a reliable source of water for firefighting purposes — allowing quick access to water and improving firefighting effectiveness.

Key Components

  • Water Source — municipal water supply, lake or reservoir
  • Pumping Station — increases water pressure in the system
  • Distribution Pipes — underground network distributing water throughout
  • Fire Hydrants — installed at regular intervals for water access
  • Valves & Fittings — control flow and connect equipment

Types

Wet riser system (hydrant filled with water at all times) · Dry riser system (drained after use, for freezing areas) · Pressure-reducing system.

Service 02

Fire Alarm System

A critical safety component that detects and alerts people to potential fires, allowing for prompt evacuation and firefighting response.

Key Components

  • Smoke Detectors — sensors that detect smoke particles
  • Heat Detectors — detect temperature increases (e.g. kitchens)
  • Flame Detectors — optical or infrared sensors that detect flames
  • Manual Pull Stations — manual triggering in an emergency
  • Control Panel — processes detector signals and initiates alarms
  • Horns, Strobes & Speakers — audible and visual notification

Types

Conventional · Addressable (identifies specific detector locations) · Wireless.

Service 03

Fire Sprinkler System

A network of pipes and sprinklers designed to discharge water in the event of a fire — providing a critical layer of protection for people, property and assets.

Key Components

  • Water Supply — municipal or private water source
  • Pumping System — ensures effective sprinkler discharge pressure
  • Distribution Pipes — underground and above-ground network
  • Sprinklers — pendant, upright and sidewall types for correct coverage
  • Control Valves — isolate sections for maintenance, testing or repair

Types

Wet pipe · Dry pipe (freezing areas) · Pre-action · Deluge (high-hazard areas).

Service 04

Fire Suppression System

Fire suppression systems are used where water-based systems are not suitable — protecting specialised hazards, equipment and spaces.

  • Design & planning tailored to the hazard class of the area
  • Equipment selection matched to building use and risk
  • Installation, testing and maintenance per manufacturer instructions
Detailed system specifications (agent types, cylinder sizes, discharge timings) require confirmation from Karma Fire Protection LLP before publication.

Service 05 & 06

Installation, Testing & Maintenance

Every system follows the same disciplined workflow — from site evaluation to annual inspection.

Planning & Design

Site evaluation, water supply evaluation and system design including pipe, hydrant, detector and sprinkler layouts — checked against applicable codes.

Excavation & Piping

Trenching that avoids existing utilities, pipe laying, hydrant and equipment installation anchored and accessible.

Valves & Fittings

Gate valves, ball valves and check valves installed, with elbows, tees and couplings connecting pipes and valves.

Testing & Inspection

Hydrostatic testing, flow testing and inspection to verify the system meets local and national codes and standards.

Final Connections

Final connections to water supply and hydrants, then a complete system test to confirm correct functioning before handover.

Maintenance & Inspection

Regular maintenance — inspecting and lubricating valves, cleaning and testing detectors — plus annual inspection visits.

Why regular maintenance matters: system reliability, prevention of system failure, reduced risk of accidental discharge, compliance with regulations, extended system lifespan, lower long-term costs, firefighter safety and business continuity.
